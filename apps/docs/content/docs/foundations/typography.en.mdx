- Headlines are Fraunces 400: never bold, never italic for emphasis. The display size scales from 48 to 96px.
- Chinese and Japanese fall back to system faces and take no tracking on headings.
- Numbers that change or line up use `tabular-nums`. Titles are balanced, paragraphs pretty, about 65 characters a line at most.
- Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
