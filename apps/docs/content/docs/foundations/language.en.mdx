Sunbeam serves fast interaction. Its language is two layers: the interface is clear, quick and precise; the background brings a humanist friction through impressionist photographs. Each keeps to its own job.

## Clear

State reads through contrast and form, not stacked colors. Chosen, on and checked are solid ink; yellow is kept for the one primary action or result on a screen. At a glance, the most important thing is yellow, the next is ink, and the rest recedes.

## Quick

Every input answers visibly within 100ms: a press sinks to 0.97, a switch's knob overshoots a hair into its detent, a popup grows from its trigger on a spring. See [Motion](./motion).

## Light

Popups enter in 160–240ms and leave about 40% faster. No decorative entrances and no loops, except Spinner and Skeleton, which mark real waiting.

## Friction

Only in the photographs: motion-blurred natural photographs under heroes, empty states, onboarding and product demos. One surface on a photograph is solid; the rest are translucent tints or run off the edge. Components carry no texture, no grain and no paper. See [Photographs](./photographs).

## Flat and raised, side by side

One language with two materials, used by layer, not picked per component: the page and the window's chrome are flat; layers floating above them are raised. See [Material](./material).
