---
title: "Copy"
description: "Say it once in the affirmative; every visible string comes in through props."
---

- Say it once, clearly. A title names the thing; a lead adds what the title leaves unsaid. Each sentence earns its place.
- Write Chinese UI copy in the affirmative, using phrases like 改用, 换用, 推荐 and 改成这样 to state the preferred choice.
- Write English UI copy in the affirmative: name the action or the alternative directly.
- Status lines are short and present tense: “Syncing…”, “Synced”.
- A destructive confirmation states the consequence in its title, “Delete Atlas?”, and names its buttons by action, “Delete” and “Keep it”.
- The brand is AstraFoundry, one word, in every language.
- Every visible string and accessible name comes in through props, such as `closeLabel`, `labels` and `characterLabel`.
