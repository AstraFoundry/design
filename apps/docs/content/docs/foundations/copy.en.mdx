---
title: "Copy"
description: "Say it once; components carry no default copy."
---

- A title says what it is; a lead adds what the title cannot. They never repeat each other.
- Status lines are short and present tense: “Syncing…”, “Synced”.
- A destructive confirmation states the consequence in its title, “Delete Atlas?”, and names its buttons by action, “Delete” and “Keep it”, not OK and Cancel.
- The brand is AstraFoundry, one word, in every language.
- Every visible string and accessible name comes in through props, as `closeLabel`, `labels` or `characterLabel`; components carry no English defaults.
