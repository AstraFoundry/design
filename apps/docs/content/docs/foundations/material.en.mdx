|                | Flat                                                                            | Raised                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Where          | The page and the window's chrome: title bars, toolbars, sidebars, content cards | Floating layers (panels, popovers, menus, dialogs, drawers, toasts) and every control in them |
| Control        | One translucent fill, no edge, no shadow                                        | A faint top-to-bottom gradient, a 1px translucent edge, a 1px top highlight, layered shadows  |
| Set of choices | A fill; the choice is a solid thumb                                             | A recessed well; the choice is its one raised face                                            |
| Text field     | Card color with a hairline                                                      | A recessed well                                                                               |
| Dark           | Translucent white fills                                                         | Faces lighter than the panel, near-black edges, half-black shadows                            |

## Rules

1. A layer has one material. A flat part inside a raised layer, as a Toolbar, opens a flat layer of its own.
2. Only controls and floating layers are raised; never text, content, page cards or a whole window. Images and thumbnails take no material.
3. Popups render into `body` through a portal, so each one carries its own `data-material="raised"`.
4. In the dark, a raised face is lighter than what it sits on and its shadow darker.

## In your own components

```tsx
<div data-material="raised" className="material-panel rounded-continuous-tile p-2">
  <button className="material-face rounded-full px-4">…</button>
  <input className="material-field rounded-lg px-3" />
</div>
```

Utilities: `material-face`, `material-face-primary`, `material-thumb`, `material-well`, `material-field`, `material-panel`, `rounded-continuous-*`.
