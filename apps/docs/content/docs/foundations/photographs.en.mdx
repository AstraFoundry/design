<Demo name="foundations/backdrop" height={360} />

- Atmosphere comes from motion-blurred natural photographs, never from gradients, glows or noise.
- One surface on a photograph is solid and crisp; the rest are translucent tints or run off the edge.
- Use a photograph as it is, with no tint, blur or vignette; choose a darker one rather than darkening a bright one.
- Text on a photograph sits on an ink scrim when it needs one (up to 55%), the one gradient allowed.
- Use the `Backdrop`, `BackdropCard` and `FloatingStage` components with the images `assets:build` renders.
